Privacy Policy for THE RIFT Bot
Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. Introduction
THE RIFT Bot ("the Bot") is a community management and utility bot for Discord. This Privacy Policy outlines how the Bot collects, uses, and protects your data.

2. Information We Collect
To function properly as a community and moderation tool, the Bot may collect and temporarily store the following data:

Discord User IDs: To assign roles, manage verification status, and track moderation actions (such as timeouts for spam).

Discord Server IDs (Guild IDs): To configure server-specific settings and architectures.

Message Content: The Bot reads message content in real-time strictly for the Anti-Link and Anti-Spam security protocols. Message content is NOT permanently stored or logged.

User-Provided Data: Information submitted voluntarily through Discord Modals (e.g., Tester Applications), which is securely sent directly to a private developer channel.

3. How We Use the Information
The data collected is used exclusively for:

Automating server architecture and role assignments.

Preventing spam, bot raids, and unauthorized links.

Processing user applications for the pre-launch phase of the game.

4. Data Retention and Sharing
We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Moderation logs (such as timeouts or deleted links) are stored temporarily in the server's private audit channels for transparency among the staff team.

5. User Rights
You have the right to request the deletion of any personal data stored by the Bot (such as previous applications). If you wish to have your data removed, or if you have any questions, please contact the developer.
